## Before claims open

During accumulation, farming rewards accrue but cannot be claimed. The team will announce opening through official channels; there is no committed opening date or maximum accumulation duration yet.

[Launch information](/launch-phases)

## Claim without resetting

Your account must retain **75% of eligible rewards earned in its active cycle**, across all PT/LP farming pools.

After earning 1,000 eligible STRIP, 250 is ordinarily claimable. If you already claimed 100, another 150 remains available without resetting. Earning another 400 adds 100 to that allowance.

Check the claim preview and choose the ordinary claim action.

## Claim more and reset

Choose the explicit reset option to claim beyond the allowance.

- Every remaining PT/LP farming deposit in your account restarts at 5% eligibility.
- A fresh earning cycle begins, with a fresh retention floor.
- Old earned rewards remain separately withdrawable and do not count toward the new cycle.
- Your principal remains staked unless you separately withdraw it.

Claiming or staking STRIP-staking fee rewards is separate from this farming rule.

## A later claim does not improve old rewards

Eligibility is applied when farming rewards accrue. At settlement, the ineligible share is minted and burned; the eligible share is held for you. Your claim transfers that eligible balance.
