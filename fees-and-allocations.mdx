The launch trading-fee split is:

| Recipient | Share of collected trading fees | How it is received |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liquidity providers | **20%** | Remains in the corresponding pool's reserves |
| STRIP stakers | **40%** | Claimable fee rewards in the collected PTs and STRIP |
| Team | **40%** | Direct revenue in the collected assets |

The reference pool swap fee is **0.3% of a trade**. At that rate, a 1,000-unit input collects 3 units of fees: 0.6 for LPs, 1.2 for stakers and 1.2 for the team. Price impact is separate from the fee.

The split applies to trading fees, not to deposited principal, STRIP farming rewards or harvested underlying yield. Check the pool's displayed fee before trading.

## Separate economic flows

- **Underlying yield:** 50% retained, 50% allocated to buybacks.
- **Trading fees:** 20% LPs, 40% STRIP stakers, 40% team.
- **Farming rewards:** 75% of eligible rewards stays unclaimed to preserve eligibility; up to 25% is ordinarily claimable after opening.

Farming emissions are allocated between supported assets and then between PT and LP staking. [How allocations work](/emissions-allocation).
