## Principal and yield

Each supported vault issues its own PT. Harvest accounting separates retained surplus from spendable buyback funds. A runner maintains supported rate observations and executes harvests and protected purchases.

## Farming

The controller allocates the fixed farming schedule to registered staking pools. Each PT/LP deposit has its own eligibility age. An account-wide ledger aggregates eligible rewards and claims across those pools.

Within a cycle:

```text
retained = eligible rewards earned - rewards claimed
required retention = 75% of eligible rewards earned
ordinary claim allowance = max(0, retained - required retention)
```

A claim beyond the allowance closes the cycle and resets the effective age of all remaining farming deposits. Old earned balances remain separately withdrawable. Historical rewards retain their historical eligibility.

Gross allocations are minted once at settlement. The eligibility discount is burned atomically; eligible tokens remain in custody until claims transfer them.

## Liquidity and fee staking

Canonical pools use 90/10 PT/STRIP weights. Their wrappers issue LP receipts. Twenty percent of collected fees stays with LPs; 40% is allocated to STRIP staking and 40% to the team.

STRIP staking distributes funded fee assets proportionally to stake during accrual. It has no farming age or retention requirement.

## Operations

Eligibility and retention are enforced on-chain. Operational runners handle harvest/rate updates, allocation updates and buyback execution. Checkpoint transactions may be required to settle long histories.
