Farming allocations follow two stages.

## 1. Between supported assets

Each asset's allocation is based on its productive capital value multiplied by its underlying yield rate: **TVL × APR**.

Values are normalized so different assets can be compared. Principal backing is counted once even when PT is represented inside an LP position.

## 2. Between PT staking and LP staking

Each asset's allocation is divided between its PT staking pool and its LP staking pool.

The team adjusts that split using trading depth and participation. Depth means the amount that can be traded within a chosen price-impact limit, considered alongside productive TVL and reward-release activity.

This gives the protocol a way to encourage additional liquidity where it is useful. It does not guarantee a particular depth, LP return or exit price.

Before an asset's LP venue is active, its active farming allocation goes to PT staking. LP allocations begin prospectively once the venue is funded and enabled.

## What changes your rewards?

Your pool's allocation, your share of stake and your deposit's eligibility all affect earnings. Allocation changes affect future rewards; previously earned rewards remain yours.

The allocation runner submits updates on-chain using validated observations. STRIP's market price does not automatically increase the emission schedule.
