---
title: "Lockless Boost"
description: "Time becomes advantage without capital becoming a hostage."
---

Strip does not use lockups to force alignment. Lockups trap capital: they make commitment visible, but they also make exit artificial. Strip uses a different signal: **sustained participation**.

### The mechanic

- Every eligible position starts at a **1x** boost.
- As alignment continues, the multiplier grows over time.
- After **60 days** of sustained alignment, the position reaches the maximum boost of **20x**.
- If alignment breaks, the boost resets to **1x**.

Boost applies to STRIP incentive distribution. A user with sustained alignment competes far more efficiently for emissions than a user who enters briefly, extracts rewards, and leaves. Capital can always exit, but the accumulated multiplier does not follow it out.

### What counts as alignment

Alignment means maintaining exposure in the parts of the system that receive incentives:

- Staking Principal Tokens
- Providing canonical PT/STRIP liquidity through staked sWLP
- Staking STRIP into [stSTRIP](/st-strip)
- Holding STRIP

There is **one alignment clock per user**, applied across every venue where that user is exposed to STRIP. Moving STRIP between aligned positions, such as staking wallet STRIP into stSTRIP, does not break alignment.

### Deliberately unforgiving

The mechanic is simple on purpose. Alignment grows over time and resets instantly. The path to maximum boost is clear: stay aligned. The cost of breaking alignment is equally clear: return to 1x and start over.

This creates a different incentive structure from lockups. Users are not rewarded for being *unable* to exit; they are rewarded for *choosing not to*. Liquidity stays free, and advantage compounds around behavior that stays aligned with the system.

### Verification

Boost is computed offchain from onchain exposure data and published onchain as a per-epoch Merkle root. Every published root is visible on the [transparency dashboard](/transparency), so users can verify that their claimed boost matches the data attested by the protocol.

<Note>
The 1x to 20x boost curve described here is the target Lockless Boost mechanic. During the initial rollout, boost is computed proportionally from total STRIP exposure; the full 60-day sustained-alignment curve activates with the boost keeper upgrade.
</Note>
