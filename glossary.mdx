title: "Glossary"
---

## STRIP

The token at the center of the Strip loop. Realized yield routes into STRIP buyback and burn, while emissions distribute STRIP to users who strengthen the system.

## stSTRIP

The staking layer for STRIP. 70% of PT/STRIP swap fees route to stSTRIP and are distributed to staked STRIP holders, paid in STRIP.

## Principal Token

A token representing deposited value inside a StripVault. Principal Tokens are the user’s claim on principal. They are not a lockup, not a maturity product, and not a withdrawal queue.

## StripVault

A vault that accepts a supported yield-bearing asset, issues Principal Tokens, and applies the protocol’s yield routing rules.

## Yield Routing

The process that directs realized yield through Strip. Half compounds back into the vault. Half is used to buy and burn STRIP.

## Buyback and Burn

The mechanism that uses routed yield to acquire STRIP from the market and remove it from circulation.

## Compounding Base

The portion of realized yield that remains inside the vault, increasing the productive base that can generate future yield.

## PT/STRIP Pool

A liquidity pool pairing a vault’s Principal Token with STRIP. The pool creates liquidity between the principal claim and the token absorbing the system’s routed output.

## PoolWrapper

The wrapper contract for canonical PT/STRIP liquidity provision. It owns the canonical LP position and issues sWLP.

## sWLP

The fungible wrapper token received by users who provide canonical PT/STRIP liquidity through the PoolWrapper. Staked sWLP earns STRIP emissions and can receive Lockless Boost.

## Emissions

Scheduled STRIP distribution to aligned participants. Emissions follow a fixed decay curve and are allocated across vaults based on productivity.

## APR × TVL

The allocation formula used to route emissions across vaults. Vaults producing more yield per dollar of capital receive a larger share.

## Lockless Boost

The boost system for sustained alignment. Eligible positions start at 1x and grow over time, reaching a maximum of 20x after 60 days. If alignment breaks, the boost resets to 1x.

## Alignment

Sustained exposure to incentivized parts of the Strip system. Alignment can come from staking Principal Tokens, staking sWLP, or staking STRIP into stSTRIP.

## BuybackBurner

The contract responsible for routing yield and fee flows into STRIP buyback and burn.

## Circulating Supply

The amount of STRIP currently in circulation. STRIP has a fixed maximum supply, but circulating supply changes as emissions distribute tokens and buyback and burn removes them.

## Net Circulating Supply

The supply measure after accounting for both emissions and burn. If emissions exceed burn, circulating supply expands. If burn exceeds emissions, circulating supply contracts.
