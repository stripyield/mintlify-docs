---
title: "What to track"
description: "Follow productive assets, fees, allocations and actual burns."
---

The useful measures connect what enters Strip with what participants receive.

| Measure | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Productive capital and underlying yield | The assets and income supporting the system |
| Retained assets and available buyback funds | Where harvested surplus is allocated |
| Current PT/LP farming allocations | Which venues receive scheduled rewards |
| Trading fees and their distribution | Income for LPs, STRIP stakers and the team |
| Buyback spend and buyback burns | Actual market purchases and tokens destroyed |
| Reward-allocation burns | Tokens destroyed through the eligibility discount |
| Eligible unclaimed rewards | Rewards owed to farmers but still in custody |
| Lifetime issuance and outstanding supply | Consumed issuance capacity and tokens remaining |

Total economic burns should show buyback and reward-allocation burns separately. Multiplying allocation burns by a market price does not measure buyback expenditure.

STRIP staking payouts should identify each reward asset. PT payouts have their own underlying exposure; STRIP payouts retain STRIP price exposure.
