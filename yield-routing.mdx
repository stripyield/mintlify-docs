---
title: "Yield distribution"
---

Strip allocates harvested underlying yield equally between retained productive assets and STRIP buybacks.

This balances investment in future income with purchases of STRIP today. The team receives its direct revenue share from trading fees, not a separate cut of this underlying yield.

## Retained yield

**50% stays in the system's productive assets**, where it can continue earning. Retaining part of each harvest can expand the assets contributing to future protocol income.

These assets are accounted for separately from buyback funds. Keeping them productive is the operating policy, rather than a promise that they are permanently immovable. Authorized treasury actions remain relevant.

Holding STRIP does not grant a guaranteed right to redeem it against retained assets.

## Buybacks and burns

**50% is allocated to buying and burning STRIP.** Execution uses available funds, liquidity and valid prices within the permitted trade limits. Funds can accumulate between purchases.

A buyback creates market demand when executed. The purchased STRIP is then burned, permanently consuming its issuance capacity.

Buybacks do not set a price floor or guarantee that burns exceed new issuance. Their scale depends on the yield available to fund them.
