Strip combines on-chain reward rules with team-operated allocation and maintenance.

## On-chain rules

Deposit age, eligible farming accrual, retention coverage, claims and cycle resets are enforced by contracts. These rules do not require a service to watch your wallet or decide whether you sold STRIP.

The lifetime issuance ceiling must cover all new issuance, including farming and non-farming allocations. Burns do not reopen that capacity.

## Team-operated decisions

The team announces claim opening, configures supported assets and venues, and adjusts PT/LP allocations using liquidity and participation information. Operational runners update observations, harvest yield, submit allocations and execute protected buybacks.

Retained yield is accounted for separately from buyback spending, but it is not promised to be permanently inaccessible to authorized treasury actions.

## Deployment permissions

Final owner, treasury, upgrade, pause and scheduling permissions will be published with verified contract addresses. Any timelocks or limits should be verified against that deployment rather than assumed from a general description.

The team's intention not to stake its STRIP allocation is a commitment, not an on-chain prohibition.
