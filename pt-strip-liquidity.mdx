Each supported vault has a canonical **90/10 PT/STRIP weighted pool**. The weights describe the pool design, not a promise that your position maintains a fixed dollar composition as markets move.

Provide PT and STRIP through the pool wrapper to receive its LP receipt, shown as sWLP. Stake supported sWLP to farm STRIP.

## Two sources of earnings

- **Pool fees:** 20% of trading fees stays with the pool's LPs through pool reserves.
- **Farming rewards:** staked sWLP receives its share of the pool's STRIP allocation, subject to deposit age and the 75% reward-retention rule.

Your direct pool-fee share starts with liquidity provision. It does not depend on having already accumulated or staked STRIP rewards.

The remaining trading fees go to STRIP stakers and the team, 40% each. [Fee split](/fees-and-allocations).

## What LPs take on

Trades change the inventory represented by your LP receipt. Your return can differ from simply holding PT and STRIP, and trading fees or farming rewards may not offset that difference.

Withdrawals and swaps use the available pool state. A sale may have substantial price impact in a shallow market. Strip does not guarantee an exit price or provide a buyer of last resort.

The team plans to seed initial liquidity. Ongoing depth comes from participants who choose to provide it, supported by fees and LP farming allocations.
