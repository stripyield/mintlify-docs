title: "Overview"
---

Strip brings yield from supported assets into a shared token economy. Depositors supply productive capital and can earn STRIP; STRIP holders can stake their tokens to receive trading-fee income.

The opportunity is to participate in a system whose income can grow with its productive assets and trading activity.

## Capital that keeps working

Half of harvested underlying yield stays in the system, where it can continue earning. The other half is allocated to buying and burning STRIP.

Retaining yield builds the productive asset base. Buybacks turn part of current income into token purchases and permanent burns. These are complementary uses of the same income, with an equal allocation to each.

Depositors retain PT representing their principal position. The yield is what they contribute in exchange for the opportunity to earn STRIP.

## Rewards for building the system

The declining emission schedule directs more STRIP toward early participation. Deposit-specific multipliers reward capital that remains staked, while the retention rule encourages farmers to keep part of their earned STRIP unclaimed.

Liquidity providers contribute an additional service: a market between PT and STRIP. They receive trading fees as well as access to LP farming rewards.

## An interest in future activity

STRIP staking gives holders a share of trading revenue without requiring them to provide liquidity themselves. Buyers can participate directly, while farmers can stake rewards they choose to claim.

The economics depend on productive assets, useful liquidity and demand for STRIP. A growing TVL or burn counter alone does not guarantee higher returns.
