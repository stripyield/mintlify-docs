title: "Claim your farming rewards"
description: "Take your available rewards or choose to claim more and reset."
---

Once claims open, you have two ways to withdraw earned STRIP. The app's preview shows whether your chosen amount preserves your farming eligibility or resets it.

## Claim without resetting

Keep 75% of the eligible farming rewards earned in your current cycle unclaimed. The remaining 25%, less any amount already claimed, is available without a reset.

Suppose you have earned 1,000 STRIP. You can claim 250. If you already claimed 100, another 150 remains available. Earn another 400 and your allowance increases by 100.

Choose the ordinary claim action and review the available amount before confirming.

## Claim more and restart your ramps

Choose the explicit claim-and-reset option if you want more than your ordinary allowance.

Every remaining PT and LP farming deposit in your account restarts at 5% eligibility. A fresh earning cycle begins with a fresh retention requirement.

Your old earned rewards remain yours. Any amount left after the claim stays separately withdrawable and does not count toward the new cycle. Your principal stays staked unless you withdraw it separately.

## During accumulation

Rewards accrue but claims are closed. The team will announce opening through official channels. There is no committed opening date or maximum accumulation duration yet. [Read about launch](/launch-phases).

## Waiting does not upgrade earlier rewards

Rewards use your eligibility when they accrue. Reaching the top of the ramp later does not increase an earlier day's earnings. The discounted portion is burned at settlement; claiming transfers the eligible rewards held for you.

STRIP-staking fee rewards have their own claim flow and are not subject to this farming-retention rule.
