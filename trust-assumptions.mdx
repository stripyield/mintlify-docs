Contracts enforce farming rewards and retention. The team operates allocations, supported venues and maintenance.

## Contract rules

Contracts track deposit age, calculate earned rewards and enforce the 75% retention rule. They also handle claims and cycle resets. A wallet-watching service does not decide whether you qualify.

The lifetime issuance limit covers farming and other new issuance. Burning tokens does not reopen that capacity.

## Team operations

The team announces claim opening, configures supported assets and venues, and adjusts PT/LP reward allocations using liquidity and participation information.

Operational services maintain rate observations, harvest yield, submit allocation updates and execute buybacks within their permitted limits.

Retained yield is kept separate from buyback funds. It is not promised to be permanently inaccessible to authorized treasury actions.

## Deployment permissions

Final permissions for ownership, treasury actions, upgrades, pauses and claim scheduling will be published with verified contract addresses. Any timelocks or limits should be checked against those contracts.

The team's intention not to stake its STRIP allocation is a commitment, not a contract-enforced prohibition.
