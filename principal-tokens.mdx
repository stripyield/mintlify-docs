A **Principal Token (PT)** is what you receive when you deposit a supported asset. Each vault has its own PT, representing a principal position in that vault.

Your asset continues generating yield inside Strip, but that yield goes through the protocol rather than to you. Staking PT gives you the opportunity to earn STRIP in return.

## What you can do with PT

You can hold or transfer PT, stake it to farm STRIP, or combine it with STRIP to provide liquidity. Holding PT alone does not earn farming rewards or pass the underlying yield to you.

PT received as a trading-fee payout works the same way as PT received from a deposit.

## Getting your principal back

PT has no fixed maturity date. If your PT is staked, unstake the amount you want to redeem and use the vault's withdrawal flow.

Redemption depends on the underlying asset, available liquidity and the vault's contract and oracle conditions. PT is not an insured or guaranteed dollar balance. Its market price in a trading pool may also differ from its redemption value.
