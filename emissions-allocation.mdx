---
title: "Emissions allocation"
---

Farming emissions reward two contributions: the productive capital behind each asset and the liquidity needed to trade it.

Allocations follow two stages. First, the protocol determines each supported asset's share. It then divides that share between PT staking and LP staking.

## Asset allocation

Each asset's share is based on **productive TVL multiplied by underlying APR**. This gives more weight to assets contributing more yield.

Values are normalized across assets, and principal backing is counted once, including when PT is held inside an LP position.

## PT/LP allocation

Within each asset, the team adjusts the PT/LP split using liquidity depth and participation.

Depth measures how much can be traded within a chosen price-impact limit. It is considered alongside productive TVL and reward-release activity. Increasing the LP share can make supplying liquidity more attractive without changing the asset's overall allocation.

Before an asset's LP venue is funded and enabled, its active farming allocation goes to PT staking. Incentives support liquidity provision but do not guarantee depth or returns.

## Your earnings

Your rewards depend on the venue's allocation, your share of its stake and your deposit's multiplier. Updates affect future rewards; previously earned rewards remain yours.

Allocation updates are submitted on-chain using validated observations. STRIP's market price does not automatically increase the emission schedule.
