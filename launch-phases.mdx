title: "Launch"
---

Strip is preparing to launch in two phases: accumulation, followed by the opening of claims and trading.

## Accumulation

When deposits open, you can deposit supported assets and stake PT to earn STRIP. Each deposit builds its age under the normal farming rules.

During accumulation, earned STRIP is recorded but cannot be claimed. The same emission schedule continues after opening; there is no separate accumulation budget.

Before trading opens, STRIP has no established market price. If you enter an FDV in the app, the resulting dollar figures show your chosen scenario, not a promised launch valuation.

Underlying yield is harvested throughout this phase. Half is retained and half goes toward the buyback budget, which can build up until trading and valid execution are available.

## Claims and trading

**The team will announce opening through official Strip channels. No opening date or maximum accumulation duration is committed yet.**

The team plans to seed initial liquidity as claims open. Other participants can then supply liquidity too. Initial seeding establishes a market, not a guaranteed depth or price.

Your earned rewards and deposit ages carry across opening. You can then claim within your available allowance or choose to claim more and reset your farming ramps.

## Principal withdrawals

The restriction on STRIP claims does not itself lock your principal. You can use PT unstaking and vault redemption, subject to the underlying asset, liquidity, oracles and contract conditions.
