---
title: "Why Strip?"
description: "Let the yield from many deposits support one shared token economy."
---

A yield-bearing asset earns income for its holder. Strip offers another choice: put that income toward a shared system and earn STRIP for participating.

That choice connects two groups. Depositors supply assets that generate yield. STRIP holders can stake their tokens to share in trading fees, while part of the underlying yield funds STRIP buybacks.

## Give growth something to build on

Half of harvested yield stays in the system, where it can keep earning. The other half funds purchases and burns of STRIP.

As more productive capital enters, the system can generate more yield. As people trade in its pools, they generate fees for LPs, STRIP stakers and the team. Early farming emissions help attract the deposits and liquidity needed to get that activity started.

Growth is useful when it produces lasting income and useful markets. A rising deposit total alone does not guarantee higher STRIP prices or returns.

## Choose the opportunity that suits you

Depositing makes sense if you prefer the STRIP opportunity to keeping your asset's underlying yield. Providing liquidity adds trading-fee income and market risk. Buying and staking STRIP gives you access to fee income without first farming the token.
