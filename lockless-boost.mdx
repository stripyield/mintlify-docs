title: "Lockless Boost"
---

#### Overview

Lockless Boost is the protocol's reputation layer. It determines each participant's share of emissions based on long-term consistency, without requiring token locks or vote-escrow mechanics.

#### Mechanics

Each account has a boost multiplier ranging from 1x to 20x. The multiplier is applied on top of base emissions, so a user at 20x earns 20 times more STRIP per dollar staked than a user at 1x.

Boost increases when an account holds STRIP, stakes PT, or stakes LP, and continues to do so without interruption. Building toward maximum boost takes weeks of consistent participation. Once earned, boost persists as long as the underlying behavior continues.

Boost resets instantly to 1x if alignment is broken during an active emission period. Alignment breaks when a user:

- Sells or transfers STRIP out of the wallet that earned it
- Unstakes or withdraws from LP positions
- Claims rewards and exits the system

Transferring STRIP to another wallet, exchange, or contract counts the same as selling, since the keeper tracks STRIP exposure per address. If you want to move STRIP between your own wallets, expect boost to reset on the source wallet. Boost is per-wallet and does not transfer with STRIP. A wallet receiving STRIP starts at 1x and builds boost from its own activity.

Market price movements do not affect boost. Only on-chain actions are considered.

#### Boost Calculation

Boost is computed off-chain by a keeper that observes account behavior across each hourly epoch. At the end of every epoch, the keeper publishes a merkle root of all account boosts to the BoostController contract. When users claim, they submit a merkle proof and the contract verifies their boost on-chain.

This means boost updates with one-hour granularity. An action taken now affects the boost that becomes claimable at the start of the next epoch.
