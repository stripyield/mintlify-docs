---
title: "Stake STRIP for fees"
description: "Receive a share of trading fees from supported pools."
---

Anyone can stake STRIP to share **40% of the trading fees** collected by supported PT/STRIP pools.

Buy STRIP or claim it from farming, then stake it. You earn according to your share of the total STRIP staked during reward accrual. There is no farming ramp and no requirement to retain 75% of these fee rewards.

## Receive the assets people trade

Your rewards arrive in the respective **PTs and STRIP** collected as fees. You can choose which reward balances to claim and leave the others for later.

The protocol does not automatically swap these payouts into a single token. Optional conversion in the interface is planned for later.

PT payouts represent principal positions in their respective vaults. STRIP payouts come from collected trading fees, rather than new staking emissions.

## What affects your return?

More trading can mean more fees to share. More STRIP staked means those fees are divided among more tokens. Fee rates and the value of the payout assets also matter, so returns vary.

Unclaimed farming rewards do not earn these fees automatically. To participate, claim STRIP and stake it. A farming claim above your allowance still triggers the farming reset.

The team intends not to stake its allocation. That is a team commitment, not a restriction on token transfers enforced by the contracts.
