Strip gives you two ways to participate: put a yield-bearing asset to work earning STRIP, or stake STRIP to receive a share of trading fees.

## Deposit to earn STRIP

When you deposit a supported asset, you receive a Principal Token, or **PT**, representing your principal position. Stake that PT to earn STRIP. You can also pair PT with STRIP to provide liquidity, earning pool fees alongside farming rewards.

In return, the yield your deposited asset generates goes into Strip. Half stays in the system to keep earning. Half funds purchases of STRIP, which are then burned.

You keep your principal position and earn a stake in the token economy that your yield helps support.

## Stake STRIP to earn fees

You don't need to deposit an underlying asset to participate. Anyone can buy STRIP and stake it.

STRIP stakers share **40% of trading fees** from supported PT/STRIP pools. Rewards arrive in the PTs and STRIP collected from those trades. There is no farming ramp on STRIP staking.

## Staying earns you more

Each PT or LP farming deposit starts with a **1x reward multiplier** that grows to **20x over 60 days**. New deposits build their own age without changing the age of earlier deposits.

Keep **75% of your earned farming rewards unclaimed** to preserve that earning rate. Once claims open, you can claim the other 25% without resetting, or claim more and restart your farming ramps.
