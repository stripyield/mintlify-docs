---
title: "Frequently asked questions"
---

## What do I give up when I deposit?

The underlying yield is routed through Strip. You receive PT representing your principal position and can stake it, or provide supported liquidity, to earn STRIP.

## Is STRIP a guaranteed higher return?

No. Rewards depend on pool allocations, your stake and eligibility. Their value depends on market prices. Any FDV you enter is a scenario assumption, not a promised launch valuation.

## What does 20x mean?

Each farming deposit starts at 1x and builds to a 20x reward multiplier over 60 days. At 20x, the same deposit earns twenty times its 1x reward rate under otherwise identical pool conditions. Actual rewards also depend on the pool allocation and your share of its stake.

## Does adding capital reset my old deposit?

No. Added capital starts its own ramp. Existing deposits preserve their ages.

## How much can I claim?

Once claims open, up to 25% of eligible farming rewards earned in your current cycle, less what you already claimed, without resetting. Keep the other 75% unclaimed to preserve eligibility.

## Can I claim everything?

After opening, yes, by explicitly accepting a farming reset. All remaining PT/LP farming deposits restart at a 1x reward multiplier and a fresh cycle begins. Previously earned rewards remain yours.

## Does holding STRIP in my wallet count as retention?

No. Only eligible farming rewards left unclaimed in protocol custody count toward the active cycle's requirement.

## When do claims open?

The team will announce opening through official channels. No opening date or maximum accumulation duration is committed yet. See [Launch](/launch-phases).

## Does STRIP staking have the same ramp?

No. Anyone can stake STRIP and earn a proportional share of trading fees without the farming ramp or retention rule.

## What do STRIP stakers receive?

Their share of collected fee tokens: the respective PTs and STRIP. Reward assets are selectively claimable. Optional conversion in the interface is planned for later.

## How are trading fees split?

20% stays with LPs, 40% goes to STRIP stakers and 40% to the team. This is separate from the 50/50 underlying-yield split and the 75% farming-reward retention rule.

## Are retained assets permanently locked?

No. Retained yield is separately accounted for and ordinarily kept productive. Authorized treasury powers remain relevant; STRIP does not promise redemption against those assets.

## Do burns guarantee the price rises?

No. Buyback burns involve market purchases; reward-allocation burns do not. Both consume lifetime issuance capacity, but market demand and selling still determine price.

## Can I withdraw principal while STRIP claims are closed?

The reward-claim restriction does not itself lock principal. Unstaking and vault redemption are subject to their own contract, oracle, liquidity and underlying-asset conditions.
