## Deposits and principal

Underlying assets can lose value, depeg or become illiquid. Smart-contract faults, integrations and oracle failures can affect funds and withdrawals. PT is not insured principal or a guaranteed dollar claim.

## Farming and claims

You give up underlying yield to participate. STRIP rewards can be worth less than that yield. During accumulation, claims are unavailable and there is no committed opening date or maximum duration.

Retaining rewards preserves farming eligibility but leaves you exposed to their value. Claiming beyond the allowance resets future eligibility across your farming deposits. External hedging is possible; retention does not prove that every participant is unhedged.

## STRIP and liquidity

Emissions can exceed burns. A large burn counter does not mean an equal amount was spent buying tokens. Trading fees and buybacks depend on activity and available funds.

LP inventory changes as people trade. LPs can underperform holding the assets separately. Shallow pools and simultaneous exits can cause substantial price impact; the protocol does not guarantee an execution price.

## Operations and permissions

Keepers and price sources can fail or become unavailable. Authorized roles administer allocations and other permitted settings. Retained assets remain exposed to their underlying and are not segregated insurance for STRIP holders.

Review [Permissions](/trust-assumptions), [Security](/audits-and-security) and the published deployment details before participating.
