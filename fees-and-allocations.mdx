### No fees on deposits, withdrawals, or yield

Strip does not charge a protocol fee on deposits, withdrawals, or harvested yield. There is no management fee and no performance fee. Principal enters through StripVaults, Principal Tokens represent deposited value, and realized yield follows the fixed routing rule: half compounds back into the vault, half buys and burns STRIP.

Yield is not taxed at the protocol level; it is routed. The treasury earns only when the system generates market activity.

### Swap fees

PT/STRIP pools charge a fixed **0.3% swap fee** on every trade. The v4 hook splits the fee inside the pool, on every swap, with no manual claiming and no governance distribution step:

| Share | Destination | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 70% | [stSTRIP](/st-strip) | Distributed to staked STRIP holders |
| 20% | Team treasury | Protocol operations and ongoing development |
| 10% | Pool reserves | Accrues to liquidity providers |

Fees are routed the moment activity happens. The split is enforced by the hook; there is nothing for the protocol to decide later.

### Two income paths for liquidity providers

The split creates a deliberate distinction. LPs earn the 10% reserve share through their PT/STRIP liquidity, and their staked sWLP competes for STRIP emissions and [Lockless Boost](/lockless-boost). But LPs are **not** automatically entitled to the 70% stSTRIP flow by providing liquidity alone; they capture it by staking earned or acquired STRIP into stSTRIP.

Liquidity earns STRIP. Staked STRIP captures protocol fee flow. This adds a layer of alignment between liquidity provision, STRIP ownership, and protocol activity.

### Emissions allocation across vaults

STRIP emissions are allocated across vaults by productivity: **APR × TVL**, so vaults producing more yield per dollar of capital receive a larger share. As vaults grow or yields move, allocation adjusts.

### Emissions allocation within a vault

Within each vault, emissions are split between two venues: **PT staking** and **staked sWLP** (PT/STRIP liquidity). The split follows the published policy described in full on [Launch Phases](/launch-phases):

- **During the Accrual Period**, 100% flows to PT staking; the liquidity venue does not yet exist because no participant holds STRIP.
- **In the absorption window after claims open**, the split moves to roughly half and half, tuned further toward liquidity if depth builds slower than expected.
- **In steady state**, the split is governed by a depth target: emissions shift toward liquidity when STRIP-side pool depth falls below 10–15% of circulating STRIP, and back toward staking when depth exceeds it.

There are no hard caps on the split; the policy states where it should sit, and the depth target is the reason for every move. What is fixed is the process: **every emission-split change passes through the 24-hour timelock**, sitting visibly onchain for a full day before it can execute. Current weights, pending changes, and the full reallocation history are on the [transparency dashboard](/transparency). See [Trust Assumptions](/trust-assumptions).

### The separation, summarized

Each flow has one job. Deposits create principal claims. Yield creates STRIP demand and collateral growth. Swap activity creates fees for STRIP stakers, liquidity providers, and operations. Emissions allocate STRIP to the users strengthening the system.
