title: "Earn fees with STRIP"
description: "Stake STRIP and receive a share of trading fees."
---

You can stake STRIP whether you bought it or earned it through farming. There is no farming ramp to complete and no farming-retention requirement on this position.

## Start earning

1. Acquire STRIP, or claim farming rewards after claims open.
2. Open STRIP staking in the app.
3. Approve the amount and confirm staking.

Stakers share 40% of fees from supported PT/STRIP pools. Your share follows the amount you have staked relative to other stakers during reward accrual.

## Claim the assets you want

Fee rewards arrive in the PTs and STRIP collected from trading. Select the balances you want to claim and confirm. You can leave other reward assets for later.

Payouts are not automatically converted or restaked. Optional conversion in the interface is planned for later; until then, you can use or trade claimed assets through available routes.

If you are claiming STRIP from farming to stake here, check your farming allowance first. Exceeding it resets your farming ramps, even when you intend to stake the tokens immediately.
