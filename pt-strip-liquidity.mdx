---
title: "PT/STRIP liquidity"
---

Providing PT/STRIP liquidity gives your capital two sources of potential return: trading fees from the pool and STRIP rewards from farming.

Each supported vault has a **90/10 PT/STRIP weighted pool**. You supply assets through its wrapper and receive an LP receipt, shown as sWLP. Stake that receipt in the supported farming pool to earn STRIP.

## Trading fees

**20% of trading fees stays in the pool's reserves for LPs.** This income starts with liquidity provision and does not depend on a farming multiplier or a separate STRIP stake.

The remaining fees are shared equally between STRIP stakers and the team, 40% each.

## Farming rewards

Staked LP receipts receive a share of their venue's STRIP allocation. Each deposit builds its own multiplier from 1x to 20x over 60 days, with the same account-wide 75% retention rule used for PT farming.

The team can direct a larger share of an asset's farming allocation toward LPs when liquidity needs support. Your return still depends on allocations, participation and market prices.

## Position value and withdrawals

The pool's weights describe its design, not a fixed dollar composition for your position. Trading changes the amounts of PT and STRIP represented by your receipt.

When you remove liquidity, you receive assets according to the pool's state. Your result can differ from holding those assets separately, and fees or farming rewards may not offset that difference.

The team plans to seed initial pools. Ongoing depth comes from participants choosing to provide liquidity. Strip does not guarantee an exit price.
