---
title: "Supply and burns"
description: "How STRIP enters the system and how tokens are removed."
---

STRIP farming starts with higher emissions that decline over time. Burns remove tokens under a lifetime issuance ceiling that cannot be replenished by burning.

## The farming schedule

The schedule starts at **900,000 STRIP per day** and declines exponentially at **0.001 per day**, within a **900 million STRIP farming budget**. The daily rate halves approximately every 693 days.

The same clock runs during accumulation and after claims open. Claim restrictions affect when rewards can be withdrawn, not the emission schedule itself.

Your deposit earns a percentage of its full reward allocation based on its [farming eligibility](/lockless-boost).

## Two ways STRIP is burned

**Buybacks:** the protocol uses its buyback funds to purchase STRIP from the market and destroy it.

**Farming discounts:** when a farming allocation is settled, the gross amount is minted and the portion excluded by historical eligibility is immediately burned. Your earned portion stays in custody until you claim it.

For example, a 1,000 STRIP allocation at 40% eligibility produces 400 earned STRIP and a 600 STRIP burn. The 75% retention rule then applies to your 400 earned STRIP: 100 is ordinarily claimable once claims open, and 300 stays unclaimed to preserve eligibility.

## What the burn counter tells you

Both categories count toward total economic STRIP burned, with the breakdown shown separately. Only buyback burns involve market purchases.

The lifetime issuance ceiling remains 1 billion STRIP. Each burn reduces how many tokens can ever remain outstanding. Tokens already minted and held for user claims still count as outstanding, even though they are not yet freely circulating.

Supply can still grow when issuance exceeds burns. A burn counter alone does not tell you the net change.
