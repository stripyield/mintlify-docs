---
title: "STRIP staking"
---

Stake STRIP to receive a share of the trading activity across supported PT/STRIP pools. **40% of their trading fees goes to STRIP stakers.**

Anyone can participate with purchased or claimed STRIP. There is no farming multiplier ramp and no 75% farming-retention requirement on this position.

## Fee income

Your share follows the amount of STRIP you have staked relative to other stakers during reward accrual. More trading can generate more fees, while a larger total stake divides that income among more tokens.

This is funded trading-fee income, not a separate STRIP emission schedule. Returns vary with trading activity, fee rates, the total amount staked and the value of the payout assets.

## Payouts

You receive the respective **PTs and STRIP** collected from trades. Select the reward balances you want to claim and leave the others for later.

The protocol distributes those assets directly instead of automatically converting them into a single token. Optional conversion through the interface is planned for later.

PT payouts represent principal positions in their own vaults. They do not themselves pass the underlying yield to you.

## Using farming rewards

Farmers can claim STRIP and stake it here to participate in fee income. Rewards left unclaimed in farming custody do not earn these fees automatically.

Check your farming allowance before claiming. A claim above that allowance resets your farming ramps, even if you intend to stake the STRIP immediately.

The team intends not to stake its allocation. This is a team commitment, not a contract-enforced restriction.
