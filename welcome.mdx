title: "Strip"
---

Strip lets you put yield-bearing assets to work earning STRIP.

Deposit a supported asset and receive Principal Tokens (PT) representing your principal position. Stake your PT to earn STRIP, with a reward multiplier that grows from **1x to 20x over 60 days**.

The yield your deposit generates supports the system you participate in. Half stays productive inside Strip, while half funds STRIP buybacks and burns.

## Earn through participation

Early emissions give participants access to the most active part of STRIP's distribution schedule. Each staking deposit builds its own multiplier, rewarding continued participation without reducing the age of earlier deposits when you add capital.

You can stake PT directly or combine PT and STRIP to provide liquidity. LPs earn a share of trading fees alongside STRIP farming rewards.

Once claims open, retain **75% of your earned farming rewards unclaimed** to preserve your multiplier. The other 25% is available without resetting. You can claim more by choosing to restart your account's PT and LP farming ramps.

## Share in trading revenue

Anyone can stake STRIP to receive **40% of trading fees** from supported PT/STRIP pools. Fee rewards are paid in the collected PTs and STRIP, without a farming multiplier ramp.

This creates two ways to participate: supply productive capital to earn STRIP, or acquire and stake STRIP to share in trading revenue.
