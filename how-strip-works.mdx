description: "From a deposit to farming rewards, trading fees and buybacks."
---

## 1. Deposit a supported asset

Strip issues a vault-specific Principal Token, or PT. PT represents your principal position; the underlying yield is routed through Strip rather than paid to you.

## 2. Put your PT to work

Stake PT directly to farm STRIP, or combine PT and STRIP in a supported liquidity pool and stake the LP receipt. Liquidity providers also receive a share of trading fees.

## 3. Build your farming eligibility

Each new staking deposit starts at 5% eligibility and reaches 100% after 60 days. Adding capital starts a new ramp for that addition; existing deposits keep their age.

Your eligible rewards are earned at the eligibility applicable when they accrue. Waiting to claim does not increase earlier rewards.

## 4. Retain rewards to preserve eligibility

Keep 75% of your eligible farming rewards unclaimed within your current earning cycle. Once claims open, the remaining 25% can be claimed without resetting.

Claim beyond that allowance and all your PT/LP farming deposits restart their eligibility ramps. Your already-earned rewards remain yours. [Reward examples](/lockless-boost).

## 5. Yield and trading create separate flows

Half of harvested underlying surplus stays in the system; half is allocated to buybacks and burns. Trading fees are split **20% to LPs, 40% to STRIP stakers and 40% to the team**.

STRIP staking has no farming ramp or farming retention requirement. [Stake STRIP](/st-strip).
