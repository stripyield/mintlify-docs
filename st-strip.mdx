title: "Stake STRIP for fees"
description: "Anyone can stake STRIP and share in trading revenue."
---

STRIP staking, also called **stSTRIP**, receives **40% of trading fees** from supported PT/STRIP pools.

Buy STRIP or claim earned STRIP, then stake it. Your share of fee rewards depends on how much you have staked relative to other stakers during reward accrual.

**There is no farming eligibility ramp and no 75% farming-retention requirement for STRIP staking.** No special status is needed to start earning.

## What you receive

Fees are distributed in the assets collected: the respective **PTs and STRIP**. You can select which reward assets to claim.

The protocol does not automatically convert your payouts into STRIP or a stablecoin. Optional conversion in the interface is planned for later; swaps would use available routes and execution prices.

PT payouts represent principal positions in their underlying vaults. They do not themselves pass the underlying yield to you. STRIP payouts are collected trading fees, not additional staking emissions.

## What determines earnings?

Trading volume, fee rates and the amount of STRIP staked all matter. More trading can increase revenue; more STRIP competing for that revenue can reduce earnings per token. Fee returns are variable.

Earned, unclaimed farming rewards do not automatically earn STRIP-staking fees. You must claim and stake STRIP to participate here.

The team intends not to stake its allocation. This is a team commitment, not a token-transfer restriction enforced by the contracts.
