Strip launches with an accumulation phase followed by the opening of claims and trading.

## Accumulation

When deposits open, you can deposit supported assets and stake PT to accrue STRIP rewards. The same emission schedule runs through accumulation and trading; accumulation has no separate farming budget.

Your farming eligibility grows with each deposit's age. Earned STRIP is recorded but cannot be claimed during this phase. Before claims and trading open, STRIP rewards have no established market price.

The app's FDV calculator lets you explore a valuation you choose. Its dollar returns are scenarios, not a promised launch price.

Underlying yield is harvested and allocated between retained assets and the buyback budget. Buyback funds can accumulate until trading and valid execution are available.

## Opening claims and trading

**The team will announce claim opening through official Strip channels. No opening date or maximum accumulation duration is committed at this stage.**

The plan is for the team to seed initial liquidity as claims open. Other participants can then provide liquidity. Initial liquidity is not a guarantee of trading depth or execution price.

After opening, you can claim within your available allowance while preserving farming eligibility, or explicitly claim more and reset. Your deposit ages and earned rewards carry across opening; opening itself does not restart your ramp.

## What you can withdraw

The claim restriction applies to STRIP farming rewards. It is separate from unstaking PT and redeeming principal through the vault. Redemptions remain subject to the underlying asset, available liquidity, oracle operation and contract conditions.
