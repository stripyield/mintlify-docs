---
title: "Fees"
---

Strip does not charge a protocol fee on deposits, withdrawals, or yield. Every dollar of realized yield from a vault is split between compounding the principal base and buying back STRIP. Nothing is charged at the protocol level.

The treasury earns only when the protocol generates economic activity. There is no management fee and no performance fee on yield.

**Swap fees in Liquidity Pools**

PT/STRIP liquidity pools charge a fixed 0.3% swap fee on every trade. The hook splits that fee three ways:

- 70% to stSTRIP, distributed to staked STRIP holders, paid in STRIP.
- 20% to team treasury, funding protocol operations and ongoing development.
- 10% to LPs, staying in pool reserves and accruing to sWLP holders as additional compensation alongside STRIP emissions.

The split is enforced by the hook on every swap. There is no manual claiming or governance distribution.
