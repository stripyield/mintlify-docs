---
title: "How Strip works"
---

Strip separates your principal position from the yield it generates. You retain PT representing that principal and can stake it to earn STRIP, while the underlying yield supports retained assets and buybacks.

## Deposit

Choose a supported yield-bearing asset and deposit it into its vault. You receive that vault's Principal Token, or PT.

PT has no fixed maturity date. You can hold it, transfer it or redeem it through the vault, subject to the underlying asset and withdrawal conditions.

## Earn STRIP

Stake PT directly, or combine PT and STRIP in a supported liquidity pool and stake the LP receipt.

Each farming deposit starts with a **1x multiplier** and builds to **20x over 60 days**. Additional deposits start their own ramps; your earlier deposits keep the age they have earned.

Your actual reward depends on the pool's allocation, your share of its stake and your multiplier when rewards accrue.

## Choose when to claim

Once claims open, leave 75% of earned farming rewards unclaimed to preserve your multiplier. The remaining 25% is available without resetting.

For example, after earning 1,000 STRIP, you can claim 250 and retain 750. Claiming more is possible, but restarts every PT and LP farming ramp in your account. Previously earned rewards remain yours.

[Full farming rules](/lockless-boost)

## Follow the income

Half of harvested underlying yield stays productive inside Strip. Half funds purchases and burns of STRIP.

Trading generates a separate income stream. Fees are shared **20% with LPs, 40% with STRIP stakers and 40% with the team**.

You can stake claimed or purchased STRIP to participate in that fee income. STRIP staking has no farming ramp or farming-retention requirement.
