title: "How Strip works"
---

Deposit a supported yield-bearing asset, receive PT and stake it to earn STRIP. The underlying yield is routed through the protocol.

## Deposit

Deposit the asset and receive its Principal Token, or PT. Your PT represents your principal position in that vault. You can hold it, transfer it or redeem it through the vault's withdrawal process.

To earn STRIP, stake your PT. Alternatively, combine PT and STRIP in a supported liquidity pool and stake the LP receipt.

## Farm

Each farming deposit begins with a 1x reward multiplier. It grows steadily to 20x over 60 days.

Adding more capital starts a separate ramp for the new deposit. Your earlier deposits keep the age they have already earned.

## Claim

Once claims open, you can claim 25% of your eligible farming rewards while leaving 75% unclaimed to preserve your earning rate.

For example, earning 1,000 STRIP lets you claim 250 without resetting. Want to claim more? You can, but every PT and LP farming deposit in your account restarts its 60-day ramp. Rewards you have already earned remain yours.

[See the full reward rules](/lockless-boost).

## Yield and fees

Half of harvested yield stays in the system. Half is allocated to buying and burning STRIP.

Trading creates a separate source of income. Pool fees go **20% to LPs, 40% to STRIP stakers and 40% to the team**. Anyone can stake STRIP for those fee rewards, without a farming ramp.
