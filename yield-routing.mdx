Strip separates underlying yield from the user's principal position.

| Share of harvested underlying surplus | Destination |
| --- | --- |
| 50% | Retained in the system's productive assets |
| 50% | Allocated to STRIP buybacks and burns |

The team receives its direct revenue share from trading fees, not a separate cut of this underlying-yield split.

## Retained yield

Retained assets can continue earning yield. They are accounted for separately from the buyback budget, so an ordinary buyback spends only its assigned funds.

Retention is an operating policy, not a promise that those assets are permanently immovable. Authorized treasury actions remain subject to the deployed contracts' permissions. Retained assets are not a redemption reserve promised to STRIP holders.

## Buybacks

Buyback execution uses accumulated funds to purchase STRIP and burn it. Purchases depend on available liquidity, valid prices and execution limits; they need not happen immediately after each harvest.

A burn permanently removes tokens under the lifetime issuance limit. Buybacks create market purchases, but do not guarantee a token price or that burns exceed emissions.
