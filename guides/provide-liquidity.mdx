---
title: "Provide liquidity"
description: "Earn pool fees and stake your LP receipt to farm STRIP."
---

1. Choose a supported PT/STRIP pool.
2. Supply the required PT and STRIP through its wrapper, reviewing amounts and slippage.
3. Receive the pool's LP receipt, shown as sWLP.
4. Stake sWLP in its supported farming pool to earn STRIP.

LPs receive **20% of trading fees** through pool reserves. This fee share does not require a farming eligibility ramp or a separate STRIP stake.

Farming rewards do use deposit-specific eligibility and the account-wide **75% retention rule**. Only the LP receipt actually staked earns farming rewards.

## Removing liquidity

Unstake the relevant receipt, then redeem it through the wrapper. Review the PT and STRIP amounts before confirming: the pool's composition may have changed since you entered.

Capital removed from farming loses its staking age. Earned rewards remain accounted for separately. Selling the withdrawn tokens is another trade at available market prices.
