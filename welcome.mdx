title: "Strip"
---

Deposit a supported yield-bearing asset to receive Principal Tokens (PT). Stake PT to earn STRIP, or provide PT/STRIP liquidity to earn pool fees and farm STRIP.

Your underlying yield goes into the protocol: **50% stays productive and 50% funds STRIP buybacks and burns**. Your PT represents your principal position.

## Farming

Each PT or LP staking deposit builds its own reward multiplier from **1x to 20x over 60 days**. Adding capital starts a new ramp without changing the age of earlier deposits.

Keep **75% of earned farming rewards unclaimed** to preserve your multiplier. Once claims open, you can claim the other 25% without resetting. Claiming more restarts every PT/LP farming ramp in your account.

## STRIP staking

Anyone can stake STRIP to share **40% of trading fees** from supported pools. Payouts are in the collected PTs and STRIP. STRIP staking has no farming ramp or farming-retention requirement.
