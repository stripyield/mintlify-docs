Strip connects productive capital with a token that earns trading fees and benefits from yield-funded buybacks.

Deposit a supported yield-bearing asset and receive **Principal Tokens (PT)**. Stake your PT to earn STRIP, or provide PT/STRIP liquidity to earn trading fees and farm STRIP. Anyone can buy and stake STRIP to share in trading fees.

## Your yield powers the system

The underlying yield from deposited assets is routed in two directions:

- **50% is retained** in the system, where it can continue generating yield.
- **50% funds STRIP buybacks and burns**, when market conditions allow execution.

Your PT represents your principal position. You give up the underlying yield in exchange for the opportunity to earn STRIP.
