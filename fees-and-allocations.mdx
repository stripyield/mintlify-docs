title: "How fees are shared"
description: "20% to LPs. 40% to STRIP stakers. 40% to the team."
---

Every trade in a supported PT/STRIP pool generates a fee. That fee is shared among the people supplying liquidity, STRIP stakers and the team.

| Recipient | Share of trading fees | What happens to it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liquidity providers | **20%** | Stays in the pool's reserves for LPs |
| STRIP stakers | **40%** | Distributed in the PTs and STRIP collected |
| Team | **40%** | Received as direct revenue in the collected assets |

## A simple example

At the reference swap fee of **0.3%**, a trade with a 1,000-unit input collects 3 units of fees. Of those, 0.6 goes to LPs, 1.2 to STRIP stakers and 1.2 to the team.

This split applies to the fee, not the whole trade or your deposited principal. Price impact is separate, and you should check the displayed fee before trading.

## Three percentages, three different jobs

Trading fees use the **20/40/40 split** above.

Underlying yield uses a separate **50/50 split** between retained assets and the buyback budget.

The **75% farming-retention rule** determines how much of your earned farming rewards you leave unclaimed to preserve eligibility. It does not change how trading fees are shared.
