title: "Emissions allocation"
---

Farming emissions are allocated between supported assets, then between each asset's PT and LP staking pools.

## Asset allocation

Each asset's share is based on the value of productive capital and the underlying yield it generates: **TVL multiplied by APR**.

This gives more weight to assets contributing more yield. Values are made comparable across assets, and principal backing is counted once, including when PT sits inside an LP position.

## PT/LP allocation

Each asset has a PT staking venue and, once active, an LP staking venue. Its allocation is divided between them.

The team adjusts this split using liquidity depth and participation. Depth measures how much someone can trade within a chosen price-impact limit. It is considered alongside productive TVL and reward-release activity.

Giving LP staking a larger share can make providing liquidity more attractive. It does not guarantee a particular LP return or trading depth.

Before an asset's LP venue is funded and enabled, its active farming allocation goes to PT staking.

## Earnings

Your rewards depend on the pool's allocation, your share of its stake and your deposit's eligibility. Allocation changes affect future rewards; rewards you have already earned remain yours.

Updates are submitted on-chain using validated observations. A falling STRIP price does not automatically increase the emission schedule.
