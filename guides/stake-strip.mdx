---
title: "Stake STRIP"
description: "Share in trading fees without a farming ramp."
---

1. Acquire STRIP or claim eligible farming rewards after claims open.
2. Open STRIP staking in the app.
3. Approve the amount and confirm staking.
4. Accrue your proportional share of the staking fee distribution.

STRIP stakers share **40% of supported PT/STRIP trading fees**. Payouts arrive in the respective PTs and STRIP, as collected.

There is no farming boost ramp or 75% retention rule on this staking position. Anyone can stake, and reward accounting follows their stake during accrual.

## Claim your fee rewards

Select the reward assets you want to claim and confirm. You can leave other balances unclaimed. Rewards are not automatically converted or restaked.

Optional conversion through the interface is planned for later. Until then, claimed assets can be used or traded through available routes.

STRIP held unclaimed in farming custody is separate from this position. Claiming it to stake may reset farming eligibility if that claim exceeds your allowance.
