Track deposits, underlying yield, fee income, rewards and burns.

| Follow | To understand |
| --- | --- |
| Productive deposits and underlying yield | What supports the system's income |
| Retained assets and buyback funds | How harvested surplus is being used |
| PT and LP farming allocations | Where scheduled rewards are going |
| Trading fees and payouts | What LPs, STRIP stakers and the team receive |
| Buyback spending and burns | How much was spent purchasing and destroying STRIP |
| Farming allocation burns | How much was destroyed through the eligibility discount |
| Unclaimed eligible rewards | What farmers have earned but not withdrawn |
| Lifetime issuance and outstanding supply | How much issuance capacity is used and how many tokens remain |

## Burns and spending

A total burn figure combines buyback burns and farming allocation burns. The breakdown matters: only buyback burns involve purchasing STRIP from the market.

Putting a dollar value on allocation burns does not make that amount buyback expenditure.

## Payout assets

STRIP-staking rewards can include several PTs and STRIP. Each PT has its own underlying exposure, and STRIP's price can change. A combined reward value is a useful summary, but the assets behind it matter too.

Verified contracts and reporting links will be listed as deployments become available.
