## Deposit

1. Open the Strip app and choose a supported vault.
2. Review the asset, underlying yield and deposit preview.
3. Approve the asset if required, then confirm the deposit.
4. Receive PT representing your principal position.

Depositing routes the underlying yield through Strip. Holding PT alone does not farm STRIP; [stake PT](/guides/stake-principal-tokens) or use it in a supported LP position.

## Withdraw

1. Unstake the PT you want to redeem, if necessary.
2. Select the vault's withdrawal action.
3. Review the amount and asset you will receive, then confirm.

Withdrawn staking capital loses its eligibility age. Any remaining staking deposits retain their age unless you trigger an account-wide claim reset.

The STRIP claim-opening restriction does not itself lock principal. Vault withdrawals still depend on the underlying asset, liquidity, oracles and contract conditions. Earned STRIP remains subject to its own claim rules.
