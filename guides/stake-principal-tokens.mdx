---
title: "Stake PT"
description: "Farm STRIP with a single principal-token position."
---

1. Deposit into a supported vault or acquire its PT.
2. Choose the corresponding PT staking pool.
3. Review its allocation, your deposit's eligibility and the reward-retention rule.
4. Approve and stake your PT.

Each deposit begins at 5% eligibility and reaches 100% over 60 days. Adding PT creates a new ramp for the addition and leaves earlier deposits' ages intact.

Once claims open, keep 75% of eligible farming rewards unclaimed to preserve eligibility. Claiming beyond the available allowance resets every PT/LP farming deposit in your account.

The app's hypothetical dollar returns depend on its displayed valuation assumption or market price. Neither token rewards nor their value are a fixed interest rate.
