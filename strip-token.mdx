title: "STRIP"
---

STRIP connects participation in the protocol with access to trading-fee income and yield-funded buybacks.

You can earn it by staking PT or supported LP positions. Once trading opens, you can also acquire STRIP directly and stake it without first farming.

## Trading-fee income

STRIP stakers share 40% of trading fees from supported pools. Payouts are in the PTs and STRIP collected from trades.

The value of this income depends on trading activity and how much STRIP is staked. It is separate from the emissions used to reward PT and LP farming.

## Yield-funded buybacks

Half of harvested underlying yield is allocated to purchasing and burning STRIP. The other half stays productive inside the system and can contribute to future yield.

Retained assets support the protocol's economics, but STRIP does not carry a guaranteed redemption claim against them.

## Supply

The lifetime issuance ceiling is **1 billion STRIP**. Of that, **900 million** is allocated to the declining farming schedule and **100 million** is reserved for non-farming allocations.

Detailed treasury, team and other allocation terms will be published before trading opens.

Burns permanently consume issuance capacity. Early emissions can nevertheless exceed buyback burns, and retained farming rewards can be released when participants accept a reset. Market demand and selling remain central to token value.
