# stSTRIP

### Overview

Stake STRIP, earn the 70% share of trading fees designated for stakers, paid in STRIP. Lockless, no minimum, no withdrawal queue.

### How It Works

Every swap in the PT/STRIP pools generates a 0.3% fee. 70% of that fee is routed to stSTRIP stakers, distributed pro-rata to staked balance and boost multiplier. 

The reward token is STRIP. Fees collected in PT are converted to STRIP through the V4 pool before distribution.

### Boost

Boost applies across stSTRIP and the other staking venues. A user holding STRIP in their wallet, staked in stSTRIP, and providing LP through sWLP shares one boost multiplier across all positions. There is one alignment clock per user, and it applies wherever they are exposed to STRIP.

### Lockless

There is no lockup, no cooldown, no withdrawal queue. Staking and unstaking are both instant. 

### Funding Source

stSTRIP rewards are funded by the 70% share of pool trading fees designated for staked STRIP holders. The amount distributed in any period is a function of trading volume in that period. Realized rewards vary with pool activity and are not fixed.
