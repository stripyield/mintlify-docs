---
title: "Why Strip?"
description: "Turn productive capital into an interest in a growing protocol."
---

A yield-bearing asset produces income. Strip puts that income toward building a common system: retained productive assets, STRIP buybacks, and markets around deposited principal.

Depositors receive PT representing their principal position and can stake it to earn STRIP. Early emissions give participants an opportunity to earn a larger share of the scheduled token distribution.

For STRIP holders, the opportunity is different: stake STRIP to share in trading fees, while yield-funded buybacks remove tokens from supply.

## Growth has to earn its place

More productive capital can generate more underlying yield. More useful liquidity can support trading and fee revenue. Retaining part of the yield can expand the assets generating future yield.

Those connections are the proposition, not a promise that price or revenue rises automatically. Assets can underperform, trading can slow, and emissions can exceed buyback burns.
