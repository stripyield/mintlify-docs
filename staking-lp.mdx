### Split

Emissions split between PT single stakers and PT/STRIP liquidity providers. The split follows a 90-day glide:

| Day | LP | PT Staking |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 0 | 5% | 95% |
| 15 | 12% | 88% |
| 30 | 28% | 72% |
| 45 | 50% | 50% |
| 60 | 72% | 28% |
| 75 | 88% | 12% |
| 90\+ | 95% | 5% |

Days 0 to 30, STRIP primarily enters circulation through staking emissions, LP allocation stays small. Days 30 to 90, the glide accelerates as STRIP becomes widely held and users move into LP positions. From day 90, steady state at 95/5.

Boost applies on both sides and carries across. A user who builds boost staking PT early keeps that boost when they move to LP later.
