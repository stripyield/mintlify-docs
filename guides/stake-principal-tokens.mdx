PT staking is the simplest way to farm STRIP. You supply one token and earn according to your share of the pool and the age of your deposit.

## Get started

1. Deposit a supported asset to receive PT, or acquire that vault's PT.
2. Choose its PT staking pool in the app.
3. Review the pool's reward allocation and your starting earning rate.
4. Approve the amount and confirm staking.

Your new deposit starts at 5% of its full reward allocation and reaches 100% over 60 days. Adding more PT gives the new deposit its own ramp, while earlier deposits keep their age.

## Know what you can claim

Once claims open, keep 75% of your eligible farming rewards unclaimed to preserve your earning rate. You can claim the other 25% without resetting.

Claiming more is an explicit choice that restarts every PT and LP farming ramp in your account. See [claiming rewards](/guides/claim-rewards) for an example.

Your underlying yield goes through Strip rather than to you. Compare that forgone yield with the STRIP opportunity, remembering that any displayed dollar return depends on a market price or your chosen valuation assumption.
