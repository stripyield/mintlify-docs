STRIP connects participation in Strip with its trading-fee economy.

You can earn STRIP by staking PT or supported LP positions, or acquire it from the market once trading opens. Stake STRIP to receive a share of collected trading fees.

## Supply

The lifetime issuance ceiling is **1 billion STRIP**, with **900 million allocated to the farming emission schedule** and **100 million reserved for non-farming allocations**. The detailed treasury, team and other allocation terms will be published before token trading opens.

Burns permanently consume issuance capacity. Burning does not let the protocol mint the same allocation again.

## Why hold or stake STRIP?

- **Trading-fee income:** STRIP stakers share 40% of supported pool trading fees.
- **Yield-funded purchases:** half of harvested underlying surplus is allocated to STRIP buybacks and burns.
- **Supply reduction:** buyback burns and eligibility-discount burns reduce attainable outstanding supply.

These mechanisms do not guarantee appreciation. Early emissions can substantially exceed buyback burns, and retained rewards can become sellable when users accept a farming reset.

STRIP does not grant a guaranteed redemption claim on retained assets.
