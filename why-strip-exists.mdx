Strip routes yield from deposited assets into retained productive capital and STRIP buybacks.

Depositors receive PT representing their principal position. They can stake PT or supported LP receipts to earn STRIP instead of keeping the underlying yield.

STRIP holders can stake their tokens to receive a share of trading fees. They do not need to farm STRIP first.

## Growth

More productive capital can generate more underlying yield. Retaining half of harvested yield keeps assets earning inside the system. The other half funds STRIP purchases and burns.

Liquidity supports trading, which generates fees for LPs, STRIP stakers and the team. Early farming emissions attract deposits and liquidity.

Higher TVL alone does not guarantee higher trading revenue, token prices or returns.
