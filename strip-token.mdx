STRIP is the token you earn by staking PT or supported LP positions. Once trading opens, you can also buy it directly.

Stake STRIP to share 40% of trading fees from supported pools. You receive the collected PTs and STRIP, without needing to build a farming multiplier first.

## What supports the token?

Trading activity provides fee income for stakers. Separately, half of harvested underlying yield is allocated to purchases and burns of STRIP.

Retaining the other half keeps assets working inside the system, where they can generate future yield. STRIP does not carry a guaranteed redemption claim on those assets.

## Supply at a glance

The lifetime issuance ceiling is **1 billion STRIP**:

- **900 million** is allocated to farming through the declining emission schedule.
- **100 million** is reserved for non-farming allocations. Detailed treasury, team and other terms will be published before trading opens.

Burned tokens consume issuance capacity permanently. Burning does not let the protocol issue the same allocation again.

Early emissions can exceed buyback burns, and farmers can release retained rewards by accepting a reset. Token price depends on demand and selling as well as the supply rules.
