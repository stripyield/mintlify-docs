### **Emissions**

#### Allocation Across Vaults

Each vault competes for emissions based on **APR × TVL**. Vaults that produce more yield per dollar of capital receive a larger share. As vaults grow, their share scales; as APRs shift, allocations adjust. The system rewards productive capital without manual rebalancing.

#### Distribution Within a Vault

Within each vault, emissions route differently depending on phase:

- **Phase 1 (first 30 days):** all emissions go to PT single stakers. This bootstraps initial TVL and gives early participants the longest window to build boost.
- **Phase 2 (day 30 onward):** emissions shift permanently to PT/STRIP liquidity providers, who stake sWLP to earn.

Boost applies in both phases and carries across the transition. A user who builds boost staking PT in Phase 1 keeps that boost when they move to LP in Phase 2.

#### Schedule

STRIP emissions follow a fixed exponential decay:

- **E(t) = E₀ × exp(−λ × t)**
- Initial rate (**E₀**): 900,000 STRIP per day
- Decay constant (**λ**): 0.001 per day
- Half-life: roughly 693 days (~1.9 years)

The schedule is set at deployment and cannot be changed.
