---
title: "Principal Tokens"
description: "Your principal position, separated from the underlying yield."
---

Depositing a supported asset creates a vault-specific **Principal Token (PT)**. Each PT represents a principal position in its corresponding vault.

You can hold or transfer PT, stake it to earn STRIP, or supply it alongside STRIP as liquidity.

## What you give up

The underlying yield is routed through Strip. It does not accrue to you simply for holding PT. Farming rewards are the separate incentive for staking PT or a supported LP position.

Compare the STRIP you expect to earn with the yield you would otherwise receive, including the [eligibility and retention rules](/lockless-boost).

## Redeeming principal

PT has no fixed maturity date. Unstake any PT you want to redeem, then use the vault's withdrawal flow.

PT is not insured principal or a guaranteed dollar value. Its redemption depends on the underlying asset and the vault's contract and oracle conditions. A PT bought or sold in a pool can trade at a different price from its redemption value.

PT obtained from trading-fee payouts has the same characteristics as PT obtained through deposits.
