title: "Prime vs Frontier"
---

Strip runs as two independent deployments. The protocol mechanics are identical across both. What changes is the asset universe each instance accepts, the risk profile that comes with it, and the token issued by each.\
\
Throughout these docs, "STRIP" refers generically to the yield token of either deployment. When the deployment matters, **STRIP** refers to the Prime token specifically, and **fSTRIP** refers to the Frontier token.

#### Strip Prime

Prime is the main deployment. It supports established yield-bearing assets with deep liquidity, extensive audit histories, and broad institutional acceptance. Prime is designed for participants who want exposure to assets with proven track records.

Listings are conservative. New vaults require demonstrated TVL, time in market, and broad audit coverage on the underlying protocol.

#### Strip Frontier

Frontier is the second deployment. It supports a wider range of yield-bearing assets, including newer or smaller-cap underlyings that wouldn't qualify for Prime. Mega's near-zero gas environment makes it economically viable to operate vaults at smaller TVL and to run higher-frequency keeper operations.

Listings are more permissive. Participants on Frontier should expect higher yields, broader optionality, and correspondingly higher risk on the underlying.

The Frontier app runs at _-url coming soon-_. The yield token issued by Frontier is **fSTRIP**.

#### Shared and Separate

Each deployment has its own yield token, its own emissions schedule, its own boost state, and its own buybacks. They do not share liquidity, supply, or rewards. STRIP and fSTRIP are entirely independent assets, each backed by its own deployment's vaults.

The protocol design, contract architecture, token mechanics, and Lockless Boost system are identical across deployments. If you understand one, you understand the other.

#### Choosing Where to Participate

- **You want exposure to majors only.** Use Prime, hold STRIP.
- **You're chasing higher yields and accept the risk profile.** Use Frontier, hold fSTRIP.
- **You want both.** They're independent. Participate in either or both. Boost and token balances on one don't affect the other.
