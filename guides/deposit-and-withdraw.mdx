---
title: "Deposit and withdraw"
description: "Receive PT when you deposit. Redeem it through its vault."
---

Depositing a supported yield-bearing asset gives you PT representing your principal position. Its underlying yield then goes through Strip.

## Make a deposit

1. Choose a supported vault in the Strip app.
2. Review the asset, underlying yield and deposit preview.
3. Approve the asset if required, then confirm.
4. Receive your PT.

To farm STRIP, [stake your PT](/guides/stake-principal-tokens) or use it in a supported LP position. Holding PT on its own does not earn farming rewards.

## Withdraw your principal

1. Unstake the PT you want to redeem, if it is staked.
2. Select the corresponding vault's withdrawal action.
3. Review the amount and asset you will receive, then confirm.

Capital you remove from staking loses its farming age. Deposits left staked preserve their age unless you trigger an account-wide claim reset.

Closed STRIP claims do not themselves lock principal. Vault redemption still depends on the underlying asset, liquidity, oracles and contract conditions. Earned STRIP follows its separate [claim rules](/guides/claim-rewards).
