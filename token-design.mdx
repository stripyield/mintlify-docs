### Principal Token (PT)

PT represents the principal value of a deposit. Each vault issues its own PT (PTsUSDe, PTsUSDS, and so on), tied 1:1 to that vault's underlying asset.

- Redeemable 1:1 for deposit notional
- Serves as the stable leg in PT/STRIP pools
- Composable for external integrations
- Vault-specific. One PT per vault, never pooled across underlyings

#### Yield Token (STRIP)

STRIP represents perpetual exposure to system-wide yield. As emissions decay, STRIP's value shifts from issuance toward buybacks. In steady state, STRIP is a shrinking claim on a growing yield base.

- Fixed supply of **1,000,000,000 STRIP**
  - **900,000,000 (90%)** allocated to emissions, distributed on an exponential decay schedule
  - **100,000,000 (10%)** allocated to team and investors
- Freely tradable and usable in liquidity pools
- Bought back continuously from realized yield, then burned

#### LP Token (sWLP)

sWLP is the ERC20 share token issued by the PoolWrapper for liquidity provided to PT/STRIP pools. It is the asset users hold after depositing into a pool, and the asset they stake to earn STRIP emissions and accrue boost. sWLP redeems back to PT and STRIP at the prevailing pool ratio.
