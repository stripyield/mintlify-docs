---
title: "Yield distribution"
---

Harvested underlying yield is split equally between retained productive assets and STRIP buybacks.

**50% stays in the system's productive assets.** Those assets can continue earning, contributing to future yield.

**50% is allocated to buying and burning STRIP.** Funds can accumulate between purchases, which depend on available liquidity, valid prices and execution limits.

The team earns its direct revenue share from trading fees. It does not receive a separate cut of this underlying-yield split.

## Retained yield

Retained assets are accounted for separately from the buyback budget. An ordinary buyback spends only the funds assigned to it.

Keeping those assets productive is the operating policy. They are not permanently immovable, and authorized treasury actions remain relevant. Holding STRIP does not give you a guaranteed right to redeem it against those assets.

## Buybacks

A buyback purchases STRIP from the market and destroys it. Under the lifetime issuance limit, burning those tokens does not make room to mint them again.

Purchases create demand when executed, but they do not set a price floor or promise to outweigh emissions.
