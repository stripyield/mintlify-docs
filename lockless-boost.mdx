PT staking and LP staking earn STRIP. Two rules determine how much you earn and how much you can claim while preserving your farming eligibility.

## Each deposit earns its own age

| Time continuously staked since entry or reset | Reward eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | 5% |
| 30 days | 52.5% |
| 60 days and beyond | 100% |

Eligibility rises linearly. The 1x–20x presentation means the same thing: 5%–100% of your deposit's full pro-rata reward allocation, not twenty times an uncapped reward budget.

Adding capital starts a separate ramp for the new deposit. Existing deposits keep their age. Withdrawn capital loses its age entitlement; staking it again starts fresh.

Eligibility applies as rewards accrue. Reaching 100% later does not upgrade rewards from earlier days. The ineligible portion is minted and burned when the allocation is settled.

## Retain 75% to preserve eligibility

Your account has one retention rule across all its PT and LP farming pools.

**Keep 75% of eligible rewards earned in the current earning cycle unclaimed.** The remaining 25% can be claimed without resetting, once claims open. The requirement grows as you earn more.

For example, after earning 1,000 eligible STRIP:

- Keep 750 unclaimed.
- Claim up to 250 without resetting.
- After earning another 400, another 100 becomes available without resetting.

STRIP in your wallet, STRIP staking or an LP position does not count toward this unclaimed-reward balance.

## Want to claim more?

Choose the explicit claim-and-reset option.

All remaining PT and LP farming deposits in your account restart at 5% eligibility. A new earning cycle starts with a fresh retention floor. Your old earned rewards remain withdrawable separately and cannot qualify the new cycle.

This reset affects future farming eligibility. It does not confiscate already-earned rewards or withdraw your principal.

## STRIP staking is separate

Staking STRIP earns trading fees without this ramp or retention rule. Claimed farming rewards can be transferred, sold, supplied as liquidity or staked as STRIP.
