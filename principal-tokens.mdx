---
title: "Principal Tokens"
---

A **Principal Token (PT)** represents your principal position in a Strip vault. You receive PT when you deposit a supported yield-bearing asset, and each vault issues its own token.

PT lets you put that position to work. Stake it to earn STRIP, or combine it with STRIP to provide liquidity and earn pool fees alongside farming rewards.

## Principal and yield

Your deposited asset continues generating yield inside Strip. That yield is routed through the protocol rather than paid to you.

Holding PT alone does not earn farming rewards or pass through the underlying yield. STRIP farming is the separate opportunity available when you stake PT or a supported LP receipt.

PT received as a trading-fee payout has the same characteristics as PT received from a deposit.

## Redemption

PT has no fixed maturity date. If it is staked, unstake the amount you want to redeem and use the corresponding vault's withdrawal flow.

Redemption depends on the underlying asset, available liquidity and the vault's contract and oracle conditions. PT is not insured principal or a guaranteed dollar balance. Its trading price can also differ from its redemption value.
