title: "PT/STRIP liquidity"
---

Supply PT and STRIP to earn trading fees. Stake the LP receipt to farm STRIP.

Each supported vault has a **90/10 PT/STRIP weighted pool**. These weights describe the pool's design. Your position's token amounts and value can change as people trade and prices move.

## Earnings

**Trading fees start with liquidity provision.** Twenty percent of the pool's trading fees stays in its reserves for LPs. You do not need a mature farming deposit or a separate STRIP stake to earn this share.

**Farming rewards come from staking your LP receipt.** The pool wrapper gives you a receipt, shown as sWLP. Stake it in the supported farming pool to earn STRIP, using the same deposit-age ramp and 75% retention rule as PT farming.

The remaining trading fees go to STRIP stakers and the team, 40% each.

## Withdrawals and risk

As trades happen, your LP receipt represents changing amounts of PT and STRIP. Your return can be lower than simply holding those assets separately, even after fees and farming rewards.

Removing liquidity returns assets according to the pool's state. Selling them afterward is a separate trade, with its own price impact.

The team plans to seed initial pools. Further depth comes from participants who choose to supply liquidity, supported by pool fees and farming allocations. Strip does not guarantee an exit price.
