---
title: "Provide liquidity"
---

Providing liquidity lets you earn a share of trading fees alongside STRIP farming rewards. Supply PT and STRIP through the pool wrapper, then stake your LP receipt to participate in farming.

## Add liquidity

1. Choose a supported PT/STRIP pool.
2. Enter the amounts through its wrapper and review the required assets and slippage.
3. Confirm the deposit to receive the pool's LP receipt, shown as sWLP.
4. Stake sWLP in its supported farming pool if you want to earn STRIP.

Twenty percent of trading fees stays in the pool for LPs. You earn that share through your LP position without needing a mature farming deposit or a separate STRIP stake.

Farming is separate: the sWLP you stake builds its own eligibility age, and earned STRIP follows the account-wide 75% retention rule.

## Remove liquidity

Unstake the receipt you want to redeem, then remove liquidity through the wrapper. Review the PT and STRIP you will receive before confirming.

The amounts may differ from what you originally supplied because people have traded against the pool. Selling the withdrawn assets is another trade at the available price.

Capital removed from farming loses its staking age. Rewards already earned remain accounted for separately.
